LIVE WELL EVERY DAY

Five Habits for a Healthier You

Small actions today can build healthier habits for tomorrow.

🥗

Eat Nutritious Food

Enjoy a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains and protein to help your body grow and stay energised.

🏃

Stay Active

Walk, cycle, dance or play a sport. Choose activities you enjoy and make movement part of your day.

🌙

Get Enough Rest

Keep a regular sleep routine and give your body enough rest to recharge, learn and concentrate.

💚

Care for Your Mental Health

Take breaks, enjoy hobbies and talk to someone you trust when you feel worried or overwhelmed.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day, especially when the weather is hot or you are exercising.
