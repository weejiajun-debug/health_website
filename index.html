A HEALTHIER WORLD

What is SDG 3?

Sustainable Development Goal 3 is one of the United Nations’ 17 global goals. It aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for everyone at every age. This includes preventing illness, supporting mental health, improving healthcare services and making essential care more accessible.
